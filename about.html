About NestMarket

We help good furniture find second homes — connecting local buyers and sellers for simple, safe and sustainable exchanges.

Browse Listings Start Selling

Furniture deserves more than one home

NestMarket started with a simple observation: great furniture is often discarded while someone nearby is searching for exactly that piece. We built a calm, local-first marketplace where neighbours can buy and sell pre-loved furniture directly — no middlemen, no platform fees, no fuss.

Every listing you see is posted by a real person in your area. You agree on the price, arrange a pickup and complete the exchange in person.

What we stand for

Zero Fees

Buyers and sellers trade directly. NestMarket never takes a cut of your sale.

Local & Safe

Meet nearby, inspect before you pay, and keep conversations on the platform.

Sustainable

Every reused piece keeps good furniture out of landfill and gives it a new life.

How NestMarket works

Three simple steps between you and your next great find.

  1. 01

    Post or browse

    Sellers snap a few photos and publish in minutes. Buyers search by category, condition and neighbourhood.

  2. 02

    Chat & agree

    Message the other person directly, ask questions, negotiate a fair price and settle on a pickup time.

  3. 03

    Meet & exchange

    Inspect the piece in person, complete the handover locally and give great furniture a second life.
