Buying and selling guides

  1. 1

    Search

    Enter what you need — “sofa”, “dining table” — and your location.

  2. 2

    Browse

    Filter by category, price, condition and distance, then open a listing.

  3. 3

    Contact Seller

    Message the seller with questions about size, age or availability.

  4. 4

    Make an Offer

    If the listing is negotiable, send an amount with a short message.

  5. 5

    Agree on Pickup/Delivery

    Choose a time and a safe place, or discuss delivery costs.

  6. 6

    Complete the Exchange

    Inspect the item, settle the agreed amount directly and take it home.
