    Frequently Asked Questions

    How much does it cost to list?

    Listing your furniture on NestMarket is 100% free. We don't charge any listing fees or take a commission from your sale.

    How do I get paid?

    You arrange payment directly with the buyer. We recommend secure cash exchanges or trusted instant mobile payment apps when you meet in person.

    Who handles pickup and delivery?

    Buyers are typically responsible for picking up the items, unless you explicitly state in your listing that you can deliver.
